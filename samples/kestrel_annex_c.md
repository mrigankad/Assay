# Kestrel supply agreement, Annex C: Price adjustment

C.1 The Contract Price for Goods listed in Schedule 2 shall be reviewed on the first Business Day of each Quarter during the Term.

C.2 Review shall reference the published HRC Index for the preceding Quarter, averaged over all trading days.

C.3 Where the Index has moved by more than five per cent (5%), the Contract Price shall be adjusted by the full movement, effective from 1 July 2026 and applying for the remainder of the three-year Term. The Q3 adjustment equals +18.0%.

C.4 Adjustments shall not be capped and shall not be subject to renegotiation except under Clause 21 (Hardship).
